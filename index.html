A live 6TiSCH network schedule

Animated schedule: timeslots across, the sixteen channel offsets down. Cells light up and pulse when their link transmits, a strip beneath shows which of the sixteen physical channels are in use as the offsets hop each slot, and the schedule changes over time: 6P transactions add and remove cells, the slotframe shrinks and grows with traffic, and cells of a node that moved go stale and are rescheduled.

A 6TiSCH network shares one slotframe like this: timeslots run left to right and the sixteen channel offsets top to bottom. Each lit cell is a link scheduled between two nodes, and every slot each offset hops to a different physical channel, so links sharing a slot never collide. My research decides which cells are allocated, when, and for how long, and keeps the schedule valid when the frame is the wrong length, traffic surges or a node moves.

About

I am a joint PhD researcher at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, and La Trobe University, Melbourne, through the Asian Smart Cities Research and Innovation Network (ASCRIN). At BITS Pilani I belong to the Department of Computer Science and Information Systems; at La Trobe I am a graduate researcher in Computer Science and Information Technology.

My thesis, Resource Allocation in 6TiSCH-based Smart City IoT Networks, asks how a low-power multi-hop network should schedule itself when traffic is bursty, topology changes and some nodes are on the move. The goal is communication that stays scalable, energy-efficient and reliable without a human retuning the schedule.

I am supervised by Prof. Nikumani Choudhury (BITS Pilani Hyderabad Campus) and Prof. Wei Xiang (La Trobe University, Melbourne).

Adaptive slotframe control

A slotframe fixed at deployment wastes energy when traffic is low and adds latency when it spikes. These papers let the frame length follow demand.

2025

FlexiFrame: Adaptive Slotframe Scheduling for 6TiSCH-Based Smart City IoT Networks

Raziur Rahman, Nikumani Choudhury, Wei Xiang, Tamoghna Ojha

IEEE GLOBECOM Workshops 2025, Taipei, Taiwan

Adaptive slotframe scheduling that adjusts the slotframe size to the traffic load, improving energy efficiency, latency and scheduling responsiveness in 6TiSCH IoT networks.

Project overview IEEE Xplore

2026

ASCP: An Analytical Model and Control Policy for Slotframe Adaptation in 6TiSCH IoT Networks

Raziur Rahman, Nikumani Choudhury, Wei Xiang, Anakhi Hazarika

IEEE ICC 2026, Glasgow, United Kingdom

An analytical model and adaptive control policy for slotframe adaptation, giving predictable latency and efficient resource use under dynamic traffic.

Project overview IEEE Xplore

Topology-aware resource allocation

Nodes near the root forward everyone else's traffic. Allocating cells by position in the routing tree balances load and prevents congestion.

2025

TARA: Topology-Aware Rank Allocation for Load Balancing in Autonomous Scheduling of 6TiSCH-based IoT Networks

Raziur Rahman, Nikumani Choudhury, Wei Xiang

IEEE ANTS 2025, New Delhi, India

Topology-aware scheduling that allocates resources by node position and traffic conditions, improving reliability, load balancing and congestion management in multi-hop IoT networks.

Project overview IEEE Xplore

Mobility-aware scheduling

When nodes move, schedules go stale and reconfiguration itself becomes a cost. These papers predict link lifetime and bound how often the schedule is rebuilt.

2026

PMAS: Predictive Mobility-Aware Scheduling for 6TiSCH IoT Networks

Raziur Rahman, Shambhavi Rani, Nikumani Choudhury, Wei Xiang

IEEE GLOBECOM 2026, Macau S.A.R., China. Accepted, to appear.

A lightweight predictive framework that estimates the residual lifetime of mobile links and feeds it into parent selection and lifetime-aware TSCH cell allocation.

Project overview Conference site

2026

MARC-6TiSCH: Churn-Bounded Rescheduling Control for Sustainable Mobile 6TiSCH Networks

Raziur Rahman, Nikumani Choudhury, Wei Xiang

IEEE ANTS 2026. Accepted, to appear.

A churn-bounded rescheduling controller that regulates 6P reconfiguration, manages stale cells, and improves reliability and latency under mobility while cutting control and radio overhead.

Project overview Conference site

Live network

A smart-city 6TiSCH network running in your browser on a 31-slot, 16-channel schedule. Readings climb the RPL routing tree one hop per scheduled cell, each on its hopping channel. A fire alarm floods one path, so 6P adds cells where queues build and releases them once traffic falls; the ambulance switches parent as it drives, its cells are relocated, and its packets jump the queue.
