About

I am a PhD Research Scholar working on resource allocation and scheduling in 6TiSCH-based smart city IoT networks at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad in collaboration with La Trobe University, Australia. My research focuses on designing scalable, energy-efficient, and adaptive communication mechanisms for low-power wireless networks.

Research Overview

  • Dynamic slotframe adaptation under varying traffic conditions
  • Centralized and distributed scheduling mechanisms
  • Mobility-aware IoT communication
  • Load balancing in multi-hop wireless networks
  • Analytical modeling of latency, reliability, and energy efficiency

Research Contributions

FlexiFrame

Centralized adaptive slotframe scheduling mechanism that dynamically adjusts slotframe size based on real-time traffic estimation. Improves energy efficiency and scheduling responsiveness under dynamic IoT workloads.

TARA

Topology-aware autonomous scheduling approach that allocates resources proportionally based on node rank and traffic load, reducing congestion and improving reliability in multi-hop IoT networks.

ASCP

Analytical model and control policy for slotframe adaptation using queueing theory and reliability analysis, enabling predictable latency and efficient resource utilization.
