ASCP: An Analytical Model and Control Policy for Slotframe Adaptation in 6TiSCH IoT Networks

Raziur Rahman • Nikumani Choudhury • Wei Xiang • Anakhi Hazarika

6TiSCH TSCH IoT Analytical Modeling Slotframe Adaptation

Overview

ASCP is an analytical slotframe adaptation framework for 6TiSCH-based IoT networks designed to support predictable low-latency and energy-efficient communication under dynamic traffic conditions.

The framework combines analytical modeling and adaptive control mechanisms to improve communication reliability, queue stability, and resource utilization in multi-hop IoT environments.

Key Highlights

  • Analytical slotframe adaptation framework
  • Latency-aware adaptive scheduling
  • Energy-efficient communication
  • Queue stability and reliability analysis
  • Adaptive resource allocation for 6TiSCH IoT networks

Lead Author

This research is led by Raziur Rahman, a joint PhD researcher at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, and La Trobe University working on adaptive scheduling and resource allocation in 6TiSCH-based IoT networks.
