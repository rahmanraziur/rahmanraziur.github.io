FlexiFrame: Adaptive Slotframe Scheduling for 6TiSCH-Based Smart City IoT Networks

Raziur Rahman • Nikumani Choudhury • Wei Xiang • Tamoghna Ojha

6TiSCH TSCH Scheduling IoT Smart Cities Adaptive Scheduling

Overview

FlexiFrame is an adaptive scheduling framework for 6TiSCH-based smart-city IoT networks designed to improve communication efficiency under dynamic traffic conditions.

The framework supports adaptive resource allocation to enhance reliability, responsiveness, and energy efficiency in low-power multi-hop IoT environments.

Key Highlights

  • Adaptive slotframe scheduling
  • Traffic-aware resource allocation
  • Support for dynamic IoT traffic
  • Energy-efficient communication
  • Improved scheduling responsiveness

Lead Author

This research is led by Raziur Rahman, a joint PhD researcher at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, and La Trobe University working on adaptive scheduling and resource allocation in 6TiSCH-based IoT networks.
