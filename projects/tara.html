TARA: Topology-Aware Rank Allocation for Load Balancing in Autonomous Scheduling of 6TiSCH-based IoT Networks

Raziur Rahman • Nikumani Choudhury • Wei Xiang

6TiSCH TSCH Scheduling IoT Autonomous Scheduling Load Balancing

Overview

TARA is a topology-aware scheduling framework for 6TiSCH-based IoT networks designed to improve communication efficiency and load balancing in multi-hop wireless environments.

The framework adaptively allocates communication resources according to network topology and forwarding demand, improving reliability, responsiveness, and energy efficiency.

Key Highlights

  • Topology-aware resource allocation
  • Autonomous scheduling support
  • Load balancing in multi-hop IoT networks
  • Energy-efficient communication
  • Improved scheduling reliability

Lead Author

This research is led by Raziur Rahman, a joint PhD researcher at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, and La Trobe University working on adaptive scheduling and resource allocation in 6TiSCH-based IoT networks.
